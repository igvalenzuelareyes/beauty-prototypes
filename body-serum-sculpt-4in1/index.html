Description

A high-performance body serum for smoother, more radiant skin. Its formula works on all layers of the skin to restore firmness, density, and evenness, revealing a body with more defined contours and a youthful appearance. The technology of this product includes the active ingredient RonaCare® Poppy SE, extracted from the red poppy (Papaver rhoeas), a medicinal plant revered for centuries in phytotherapy for its restorative action, which improves skin firmness and elasticity. The active ingredients RonaCare® Cyclopeptide-5 and RonaCare® Epitengrity work together in deep restructuring, strengthening the skin matrix, stimulating renewal, and improving the appearance of visible imperfections. The product's color is achieved through the Xirona® Le Rouge effect pigment, which translates the softness of the poppy flower into color, conveying femininity, sophistication, and a delicate yet resilient beauty. It is age-old beauty translated into a cosmetic active ingredient, combined with technology for effective skin care.

Procedure

Disperse Alphapol 600 in water and adjust the pH to 6.0–7.0. Add Phase B2 to Phase B1 and mix thoroughly. Slowly pour Phase B into Phase A while stirring, then add Phase C.

Notes

  • Appearance: Liquid with an iridescent effect
  • pH: 6.0–7.0
  • Stability: 3 months at 40°C, 5°C and RT.
