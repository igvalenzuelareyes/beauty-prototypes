Cream to Powder Deodorant

SBR-006-02-26

Description

A gentle, multifunctional cream deodorant formulated to care for the areas that need it most: underarms, groin, breasts, feet, inner thighs, and any sensitive area prone to friction, sweat, or odor.

With cream-to-powder technology, its texture dries quickly and leaves a velvety touch thanks to Ronaflair® Extender W and Ronaflair® Satin fillers, creating a comfortable shield against friction so you can move with more confidence. The active ingredient Ronacare® Malic Acid promotes intelligent acidification on the skin's surface, creating an environment less favorable to the growth of odor-causing bacteria.

To restore, even out, and soothe, the formula combines Ronacare® Nicotinamide, Ronacare® Bisabolol, and Emblica®—together, these active ingredients promote continuous restorative care, leaving skin softer, more even, and comfortable.

It's protection. It's care. It's freedom to move—with calm, even, and confident skin all day long.

Procedure

Disperse phase A1 without heating. Add phase A2 and begin heating to 75°C. Heat phase B in a separate container to 75°C. Pour B into A and stir vigorously for 5 minutes. Allow to stand for 10 minutes with gentle stirring, then begin cooling. Once below 40°C, add the pre-solution of C, followed by phases D and E one at a time. Cool with gentle stirring.

Notes

  • Appearance: White cream
  • pH: 4.5–5.0
  • Stability: 3 months at 40°C, 5°C and RT.
