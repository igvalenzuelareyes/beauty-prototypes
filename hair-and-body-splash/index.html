Beauty Flows Through Your Body

Hair & Body Splash - An ode to breathe

SBR-004-02-26

Description

A multifunctional splash for hair and skin that transforms daily care into a ritual of presence. Its formula perfumes and envelops the body with a touch of freshness, while the active ingredient Ronacare® AP delivers antioxidant and anti-pollution action, protecting the skin against free radical damage and urban stress. The color inspires softness and contemplation with a gentle and ethereal tone, inspired by the semi-translucent Cloud Dancer with a fruity shimmer, created uniquely by the Xirona® Golden Sky, pigment, bringing nuances of golden, blue, violet, and green tones, like an ethereal reflection in the air. A sensory choice that transports you to the serenity of a meditation in the Himalayas: one that purifies from within while illuminating from without. Perfumes. Hydrates. Comforts.

Procedure

Disperse Alphapol 600 in water and adjust the pH to 6.0–7.0. Add Phase B2 to Phase B1 and mix thoroughly. Slowly pour Phase B into Phase A while stirring, then add Phase C.

Notes

  • Appearance: Liquid with an iridescent effect
  • pH: 6.0–7.0
  • Stability: 3 months at 40°C, 5°C and RT.
