Light Essence Sunscreen SPF50 – Aurea UV Shield

SBR-001-01-26

Description

A luminous sunscreen essence formulated to deliver broad-spectrum SPF50 protection while enhancing skin radiance. Enriched with Eusolex® OCR, Eusolex® 9020, Eusolex® OS, Eusolex® BEMT, and Eusolex® EHT, this formula provides advanced UV defense with a lightweight, gliding texture. The golden shimmer from Colorona® Sun Gold Sparkle MP-29 creates an illuminating finish. Complemented with RonaCare® AP and Oxynex® ST Liquid, the essence helps protect against environmental stressors.
