Description

This body shower oil blends science and sensoriality to transform the bathing moment into an advanced care ritual. Its formula strengthens the skin barrier, enhances moisture retention, and leaves the skin immediately softer and smoother. Enriched with RonaCare® Bisabolol, known for its soothing and calming benefits that help maintain comfort and softness during cleansing, and Oxynex® ST Liquid, a powerful antioxidant with anti-pollution activity that protects against environmental stressors. The Colorona® Tangerine Orange pigment delivers a vibrant orange hue that awakens vitality. Its luminous texture conveys immediate energy, bringing light, warmth and movement to both body and mind. With a complete holistic synergy, the product helps reduce signs of fatigue and the effects of external aggressions, revitalizing the overall appearance of the skin with continuous use. A touch of high performance combined with an emotional boost of well-being — all in a single daily gesture.

Procedure

Weigh Phase A and homogenize to 60°C. Then begin cooling. Add Phase B to Phase A while stirring at moderate to high speed. Add Phase C in a spray stream, and continue stirring for 10–15 minutes. Add Phases D and E and homogenize slowly.
